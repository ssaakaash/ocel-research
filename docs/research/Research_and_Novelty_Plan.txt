TEAM 1 — RESEARCH AND NOVELTY PLAN

Project title
Object-Centric Event Log Mining for Academic Capstone Processes: A Native OCEL 2.0 Approach

1. Defensible novelty position

Do not claim that this is the first use of OCEL in education. Miri et al. (2026) already present an object-centric approach to multi-entity educational process mining and export OCEL 2.0 from Moodle.

The defensible contribution is narrower and stronger:

• A native OCEL 2.0 model for the transactional capstone lifecycle, connecting projects, teams, students, faculty, review stages, submissions, versioned artifacts, attempts, and immutable scores.
• A documentation-grounded validation triad: normative process model, descriptive process mining, and adversarial synthetic cases.
• State-machine conformance rules for capstone-specific constraints, including approval-before-submission, stage order, score immutability, attempt limits, and guide/reviewer capacity.
• A representation-preservation study comparing OCEL with conventional case-centric flattenings.
• Cross-Object Observability Coverage (COOC), a study-specific metric: the fraction of preregistered cross-object predicates that a representation can compute and answer correctly on an oracle-labelled benchmark.
• An offline digital-twin experiment for evaluating attempt-policy and faculty-capacity interventions before deployment.

2. Claim ladder

Review 2 — proof-of-mechanism claims supported by synthetic data

• The implementation produces schema-valid, referentially consistent OCEL 2.0 SQLite data.
• A native object-centric representation preserves all tested event–object relations, while the tested flattenings discard substantial relation context.
• Capstone-specific conformance rules detect the injected synthetic violations with high accuracy.
• The offline simulator can compare policy alternatives under controlled assumptions.

Claims that must remain explicitly provisional

• Detector accuracy on real Educational Capstone Portal logs.
• Predictive performance on future capstone cohorts.
• Causal impact of the simulated interventions.
• Generality of COOC outside the preregistered capstone predicates.

Future journal claims — only after real-data validation

• Native OCEL improves cross-object diagnostic coverage over case-centric baselines on real capstone data.
• Documentation-grounded conformance checks find operationally meaningful deviations with acceptable expert agreement.
• Capacity- and attempt-aware interventions improve prospective outcomes in a pilot or credible quasi-experimental evaluation.

3. Research questions and hypotheses

RQ1. Which capstone relations and constraints become unobservable when a multi-entity lifecycle is flattened to a single case notion?
H1. Native OCEL will achieve higher preregistered COOC than project-, student-, or artifact-centric flattenings.

RQ2. Can documentation-derived invariants identify capstone process deviations across interacting objects?
H2. Object-aware conformance rules will outperform single-case rules on cross-object violations such as reviewer conflicts, version lineage, and capacity overload.

RQ3. Which intervention combinations best reduce failure and waiting without creating new bottlenecks?
H3. Joint attempt-policy and capacity interventions will dominate either intervention alone on completion rate, subject to workload constraints.

4. Evidence required for a journal submission

• Preregister the object schema, anomaly catalogue, predicates, metrics, and analysis plan before examining outcome labels.
• Replace or augment synthetic data with de-identified Educational Capstone Portal event data and documentation-derived ground truth.
• Obtain independent domain-expert labels; report inter-rater agreement and adjudication.
• Run multiple seeds, cohort sizes, anomaly rates, missingness levels, and sensitivity analyses.
• Compare against event-level, project-case, student-case, and artifact-case baselines.
• Include ablations for object relations, temporal attributes, documentation rules, and source instrumentation.
• Separate Source Instrumentation Coverage from Representation Preservation Coverage.
• Release a privacy-safe benchmark generator, oracle labels, configuration, and evaluation scripts.
• If possible, evaluate on a second institution or a second multi-entity academic workflow.
