Safe wording for Q&A

• Say “on the synthetic benchmark,” “under the simulator assumptions,” or “proof of mechanism” before quoting results.
• Do not claim “first OCEL in education.” The novelty is the transactional capstone domain, documentation-grounded validation triad, cross-object conformance, COOC study metric, and offline intervention evaluation.
• COOC is a study-specific evaluation metric, not yet a universal OCEL quality measure.
• The next validation stage requires de-identified real logs, independent expert labels, stronger baselines, and sensitivity analysis.
